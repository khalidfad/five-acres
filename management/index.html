Profile

Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen is the founder and Managing Director of Five Acres Ltd. He began in information technology, moved into agricultural commodity exports, and spent 16 years at Starlink Global & Ideal Ltd before building his own company.

He leads the company's strategy, business development, sourcing relationships and market expansion, with a focus on a dependable, professionally managed commodity business.

“My journey from information technology to agricultural commodity exports has taught me the importance of innovation, disciplined execution, teamwork, and trusted relationships.”
