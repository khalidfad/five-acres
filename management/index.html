Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen

Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer, Five Acres Ltd, Lagos, Nigeria

Technology professionalCommodity industry executiveEntrepreneur

Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen is a vibrant, creative and forward-thinking entrepreneur whose professional journey spans information technology, business support management, agricultural commodity exports and enterprise development.

Executive profile

As the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Five Acres Ltd, a Nigerian agricultural commodity company headquartered in Lagos, he brings together technical expertise, corporate leadership, commercial experience and an entrepreneurial vision focused on building an efficient and dependable agricultural commodity business.

A graduate of Computer Science from Yaba College of Technology, Lagos, and a Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA), Ajani began his professional career in the information technology sector before moving into agricultural commodity exports. His diverse experience has shaped a leadership approach that combines analytical thinking, operational discipline, relationship management and a strong commitment to business growth.

My journey from information technology to agricultural commodity exports has taught me the importance of innovation, disciplined execution, teamwork, and trusted relationships.Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen

Professional journey

Systemplus Ltd, Lagos: Information technology and business support

Ajani began his career at Systemplus Ltd, a Lagos-based Internet Service Provider (ISP), where he developed his expertise in network infrastructure, internet services, technical operations and customer support.

During his tenure, he led teams responsible for deploying Wide Area Networks (WANs) and internet connectivity solutions for corporate offices and university campuses across Nigeria. These projects required technical coordination, teamwork, problem-solving, service delivery management, and the ability to meet the operational needs of diverse institutional clients.

He eventually rose to the position of Business Support Manager, gaining valuable experience in coordinating technical services, supporting business operations, managing customer relationships and leading teams toward defined performance objectives. This foundation in technology and business support established the operational and managerial capabilities that would later support his move into the agricultural commodity industry.

Starlink Global & Ideal Ltd: Agricultural commodity exports

Building on his corporate experience, Ajani moved into the agricultural commodity export sector, where he spent 16 years of his active career at Starlink Global & Ideal Ltd, a prominent indigenous Nigerian commodity export company. During this period he gained extensive industry experience and progressed to managerial level.

His career in the commodity sector broadened his understanding of agricultural sourcing, supply chain coordination, commercial relationships, operational management and the dynamics of the commodity export business. It gave him a strong foundation for understanding the relationships between agricultural producers, local buying networks, commodity suppliers, exporters and international markets.

The combination of long-term industry exposure and managerial responsibility strengthened his appreciation of quality, supplier reliability, commercial discipline and effective execution in agricultural trade.

The founding of Five Acres Ltd

From industry experience to entrepreneurship

Driven by his desire to apply his industry knowledge and entrepreneurial vision, Ajani founded Five Acres Ltd. The business subsequently developed into a full-fledged agricultural commodity company in 2025, an important stage in its evolution and commercial development.

As Founder and Managing Director/CEO, he leads the company's strategic direction, business development, sourcing relationships and market expansion initiatives. Five Acres Ltd focuses on agricultural commodity sourcing, aggregation, trading and supply, with products including:

  • Cocoa beans
  • Cashew nuts
  • Soya beans
  • Sesame seeds
  • Other agricultural produce, subject to market demand and sourcing capacity

Under his leadership, the company aims to develop dependable relationships with farmers, local buying agents, aggregators, processors, traders and commodity off-takers in Nigeria and international markets. His long-term ambition is to build Five Acres Ltd into a professionally managed, trusted and commercially sustainable participant in Nigeria's agricultural commodity value chain.

Leadership philosophy

Ajani believes that sustainable business growth is built on integrity, teamwork, innovation, accountability and dependable relationships.

His experience across technology and commodity exports has reinforced his belief in the value of structured systems, effective communication, operational efficiency and continuous improvement.

As a leader, he seeks to build strong teams, cultivate lasting commercial partnerships, and create business processes that support reliability, customer confidence and long-term growth.

Vision for Five Acres Ltd

Ajani's vision is to position Five Acres Ltd as a respected Nigerian agricultural commodity company, connecting local supply networks with credible domestic and international buyers.

He is committed to pursuing growth through strategic partnerships, responsible sourcing, quality-conscious operations, stronger supply chain relationships and continuous business development.

By combining his technology background, 16 years of experience at Starlink Global & Ideal Ltd and entrepreneurial drive, he aims to contribute to the development of Nigeria's agricultural commodity sector and expand opportunities for the company's participation in international trade.
