Executive profile

Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen is a vibrant, creative and forward-thinking entrepreneur whose journey spans information technology, business support management, agricultural commodity exports and enterprise development.

As Managing Director and CEO of Five Acres Ltd, a Nigerian agricultural commodity company headquartered in Lagos, he combines technical expertise, corporate leadership and commercial experience in building an efficient, dependable commodity business.

A Computer Science graduate of Yaba College of Technology, Lagos, and a Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA), he began in information technology before moving into agricultural commodity exports. His leadership blends analytical thinking, operational discipline and relationship management with a strong commitment to growth.

Professional journey

From networks and internet services to agricultural commodity exports and, finally, to building his own company. Select a role to read more.

Ajani began his career at Systemplus Ltd, a Lagos-based Internet Service Provider, developing expertise in network infrastructure, internet services, technical operations and customer support.

He led teams deploying Wide Area Networks (WANs) and internet connectivity for corporate offices and university campuses across Nigeria, and rose to Business Support Manager, coordinating technical services, managing customer relationships and leading teams toward defined performance objectives.

Ajani spent 16 years of his active career at Starlink Global & Ideal Ltd, a prominent indigenous Nigerian commodity export company, progressing to managerial level.

The experience broadened his understanding of agricultural sourcing, supply chain coordination, commercial relationships and operational management, and of how producers, local buying networks, suppliers, exporters and international markets connect.

Drawing on his industry knowledge, Ajani founded Five Acres Ltd and, in 2025, developed it into a full-fledged agricultural commodity company. He leads its strategic direction, business development, sourcing relationships and market expansion.

The company focuses on sourcing, aggregation, trading and supply of cocoa beans, cashew nuts, soya beans and sesame seeds, along with other agricultural produce subject to market demand and sourcing capacity.

Leadership philosophy

Ajani believes that sustainable business growth is built on integrity, teamwork, innovation, accountability and dependable relationships.

His experience across technology and commodity exports has reinforced his belief in structured systems, effective communication, operational efficiency and continuous improvement.

As a leader, he seeks to build strong teams, cultivate lasting commercial partnerships and create business processes that support reliability, customer confidence and long-term growth.

Five guiding principles

  • Integrity
  • Teamwork
  • Innovation
  • Accountability
  • Dependable relationships

Vision for Five Acres Ltd

Ajani's vision is to position Five Acres Ltd as a respected Nigerian agricultural commodity company, connecting local supply networks with credible domestic and international buyers.

Under his leadership, the company aims to build dependable relationships with farmers, local buying agents, aggregators, processors, traders and commodity off-takers in Nigeria and international markets.

How the company will grow

  • Strategic partnerships
  • Responsible sourcing
  • Quality-conscious operations
  • Stronger supply chain relationships
  • Continuous business development
