About Five Acres Ltd

A Nigerian agricultural commodity sourcing, aggregation, trading and supply company built on quality, reliability and integrity.

Company profileLagos, Nigeria8 minute read

Five Acres Ltd was established in 2023 with a clear ambition: to become one of Nigeria's most trusted and sought-after agricultural commodity companies. It entered full operational activity in November 2025, and today it supplies cocoa, cashew nuts, soybeans and sesame seeds, alongside technology: CCTV cameras and laptops.

Executive summary

The company was established in response to a significant opportunity in Nigeria's agricultural value chain: the need for reliable, professional and quality-focused commodity companies that connect farmers and local suppliers with processors, manufacturers, exporters, international commodity buyers and other strategic off-takers.

Five Acres Ltd focuses primarily on four strategic commodities, chosen for their importance to Nigeria's agricultural economy, their availability across different parts of the country, their strong domestic and international demand, and their potential for value creation across the supply chain.

Company background

Five Acres Ltd was conceived in 2023 as an agricultural commodity enterprise, with the vision of building a commercially sustainable business around Nigeria's abundant agricultural resources.

The founders recognised that Nigeria has considerable agricultural potential, but that the commodity value chain still faces challenges of fragmentation, quality consistency, aggregation, market access, logistics, information flow and dependable supply. Smallholder farmers and local producers frequently operate in fragmented markets, while processors, exporters and international buyers need dependable volumes, consistent quality, traceability and professional counterparties.

Five Acres Ltd intends to bridge this gap. After its establishment, the company developed its business model, market relationships and operational structure, and became fully operational in November 2025. It is now focused on building a scalable commodity business with nationwide sourcing capacity and a strong reputation for reliability, quality, transparency, professionalism and consistent supply.

Our business

Five Acres Ltd operates primarily as an agricultural commodity sourcing and trading company. Our model is built around identifying commodities at their points of abundance, establishing relationships with producers and local aggregators, consolidating volumes, applying quality-control procedures, coordinating logistics and supplying reliable buyers.

We also supply technology, specifically CCTV cameras and laptops. Learn more about our technology offering.

Quality management

Quality is one of the foundations of our business. Buyers are increasingly interested not only in quantity, but in consistent quality, documentation, traceability and reliability. Our approach includes:

Incoming quality inspection
Commodities are assessed before acceptance based on applicable product requirements.
Moisture management
Moisture levels are monitored to minimise quality deterioration during storage and transportation.
Defect management
For relevant commodities, defect levels are monitored and controlled.
Bean count
For cocoa and applicable commodities, bean-count information is recorded as part of quality assessment.
Foreign matter
We seek to minimise foreign materials and contamination.
Proper storage
Appropriate storage practices are used to preserve commodity quality.
Documentation
Quality information is documented to support transparency with our commercial partners.
Traceability
Where commercially and operationally appropriate, we intend to improve traceability from aggregation point to final buyer.
Our quality promiseWe source responsibly, inspect carefully, aggregate professionally and deliver reliably.

We work to ensure that every commodity supplied meets the agreed commercial and quality specifications of the buyer. In international commodity markets, reputation is built shipment by shipment.

Buyers and suppliers

Our relationship with buyers

We develop long-term relationships based on trust, consistency, transparency, quality, competitive pricing, reliable communication, delivery performance, problem-solving and continuous improvement. Our objective is for buyers to see Five Acres Ltd as a dependable extension of their procurement operations in Nigeria.

Our relationship with suppliers

We are building a supplier ecosystem based on fairness, respect and transparency. Suppliers should understand what we require, the quality standards expected, how pricing works, delivery and payment arrangements, quality deductions where applicable, and documentation requirements. Transparent relationships encourage better quality and stronger long-term supply.

Business ethics

We are committed to conducting business responsibly, and we do not seek short-term gains at the expense of long-term reputation. Our principles include:

  • No deliberate misrepresentation of commodity quality
  • Transparent commercial documentation
  • Responsible supplier engagement
  • Proper financial records
  • Respect for applicable laws and regulations
  • Professional treatment of business partners
  • Zero tolerance for fraudulent transactions

Our reputation is one of the company's most valuable assets.

Conclusion

Five Acres Ltd is a young Nigerian agricultural commodity company with a clear and ambitious direction. Established in 2023 and fully operational from November 2025, it is building its business around four strategic commodities: cocoa, cashew, soybeans and sesame seeds.

Our strategy is to develop a nationwide sourcing and aggregation network, build strong relationships with farmers and local buying agents, maintain rigorous quality standards, create reliable logistics channels and supply domestic and international buyers with consistency.

Our greatest ambition goes beyond turnover. We want Five Acres Ltd to become a trusted name in agricultural commodity sourcing: a company that farmers want to sell to, buyers want to buy from, financial institutions want to support and international partners want to work with.
