About Five Acres Ltd

A Nigerian agricultural commodity company built on reliability, quality and transparency.

Our story

Five Acres Ltd was founded to bring dependable supply and professional standards to Nigeria's agricultural commodity trade.

Farmers and local producers often sell into fragmented markets, while buyers need consistent volumes and quality. We bridge that gap, sourcing where commodities are abundant and delivering where the market needs them.

Our vision

To become Nigeria's most sought-after and trusted agricultural commodity sourcing and supply company, recognised for quality, reliability, integrity and consistent delivery.

Our mission

To responsibly source, aggregate, manage and supply high-quality Nigerian agricultural commodities while creating sustainable value for all stakeholders.

Our values

Integrity

Honesty, transparency and accountability in every deal.

Quality

Quality standards maintained from sourcing through delivery.

Reliability

A dependable partner that buyers and suppliers can trust.

Professionalism

Structured systems, documentation, market intelligence and disciplined execution.

Transparency

Clear commercial relationships with farmers, agents, suppliers, buyers and financial partners.

Partnership

Mutually beneficial relationships rather than short-term transactions.

Innovation

Technology, data and better processes to make sourcing more efficient and transparent.

Sustainability

Responsible production, environmental protection and the welfare of farming communities.
