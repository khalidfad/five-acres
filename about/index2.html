About Five Acres Ltd

A Nigerian agricultural commodity company built on quality, reliability and integrity.

Company profileLagos, Nigeria5 minute read

Five Acres Ltd was founded to bring dependable supply and professional standards to Nigeria's agricultural commodity trade. We source, aggregate and supply cocoa, cashew nuts, soybeans and sesame seeds, and we supply technology: CCTV cameras and laptops.

Our story

Nigeria has considerable agricultural potential, yet farmers and local producers often sell into fragmented markets, while buyers need consistent volumes, quality and documentation they can rely on.

Five Acres Ltd was built to bridge that gap: sourcing where commodities are abundant, aggregating them efficiently, and delivering where the market needs them. In 2025 the company developed into a full-fledged commodity business, led by its founder and Managing Director, Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen.

To become Nigeria's most sought-after and trusted agricultural commodity sourcing and supply company, recognised for quality, reliability, integrity and consistent delivery.Our vision

Our mission

To responsibly source, aggregate, manage and supply high-quality Nigerian agricultural commodities while creating sustainable value for all stakeholders: farmers, suppliers, buyers and our own team.

We work across five producing regions, building relationships with farmers, local buying agents and aggregators, and delivering to processors, exporters and international buyers.

What we stand for

Eight values guide how we deal with everyone along the value chain.

Integrity
Honesty, transparency and accountability in every deal.
Quality
Quality standards maintained from sourcing through delivery.
Reliability
A dependable partner that buyers and suppliers can trust.
Professionalism
Structured systems, documentation, market intelligence and disciplined execution.
Transparency
Clear commercial relationships with farmers, agents, suppliers, buyers and financial partners.
Partnership
Mutually beneficial relationships rather than short-term transactions.
Innovation
Technology, data and better processes to make sourcing more efficient and transparent.
Sustainability
Responsible production, environmental protection and the welfare of farming communities.
Our quality promiseWe source responsibly, inspect carefully, aggregate professionally and deliver reliably.

Leadership and technology

Five Acres Ltd is led by a technology professional with sixteen years in Nigerian commodity exports. That background shapes how we operate, and it is why we also supply technology: CCTV cameras and laptops.
