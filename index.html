Who we are

Five Acres Ltd is a Nigerian agricultural commodity company. We source, aggregate and supply quality commodities, connecting farmers and local suppliers with processors, exporters and international buyers.

Our aim is simple: to be the most trusted name in Nigerian commodity sourcing, known for quality, reliability and integrity.

Learn more about us

Meet our leadership

Five Acres Ltd is led by its founder, Ajani Abidemi Nurudeen, Managing Director and CEO. A computer science graduate and CCNA-certified technology professional, he spent 16 years in Nigerian agricultural commodity exports before building Five Acres Ltd.

His focus is a dependable, professionally managed commodity business built on integrity, teamwork and trusted relationships.
